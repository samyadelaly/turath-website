About TURATH Egypt (عن مصنع تراث)

TURATH is a premier Egyptian manufacturer specializing in authentic, high-end handcrafted brass, copper, and decorative metal products. Located in the heart of historic Cairo on Gamaliya Street, our master coppersmiths preserve centuries-old Mamluk, Ottoman, and Islamic metalworking traditions while executing bespoke contemporary designs for luxury hotels, private palaces, and residential villas worldwide.

Our Handcrafted Brass Collections (أقسام وتشكيلات النحاس)

  • 1. Brass Mirrors (مرايا نحاس مفرغة ومنقوشة)

    Sunburst, arched, and geometric hand-hammered brass mirrors combining repoussé techniques, intricate openwork filigree, and polished glass.

  • 2. Pierced Brass Chandeliers & Pendants (ثريات ونجف نحاسي ضخم)

    Sculptural pierced brass chandeliers created for grand halls, palaces, luxury dining venues, and boutique hotels, casting mesmerizing ambient shadow patterns.

  • 3. Wall Lights & Appliques (أبليكات وإضاءة جدارية)

    Artisan brass sconces designed with traditional Gamaliya perforations that transform plain walls into radiant architectural canvases.

  • 4. Engraved Tables & Luxury Consoles (طاولات وكونسول نحاس)

    Solid brass structural bases, hand-hammered brass trays, and inlaid consoles paired with marble or tempered glass for timeless living spaces.

  • 5. Solid Cast Door Handles & Hardware (مقابض أبواب نحاس)

    Heavy architectural door pulls, traditional lion-head knockers, and bespoke forged brass levers that make every entrance unforgettable.

  • 6. Architectural Brass Handrails & Balustrades (درابزين ودرج نحاسي)

    Precision-forged brass handrails, spiral balustrades, and geometric guardrails engineered to architect specifications for commercial and residential landmarks.

  • 7. Heritage Lanterns & Custom Lighting (فوانيس وقناديل تراثية)

    Authentic Mamluk and Ottoman inspired lanterns pierced with intricate calligraphy and arabesques, creating atmospheric heritage lighting.

Bespoke Architectural & Hotel Manufacturing (تصنيع مخصص للمشاريع)

We partner directly with interior architects, luxury hoteliers, and private homeowners to fabricate custom brass fixtures from initial sketches and CAD drawings to hand-finished installation pieces.
