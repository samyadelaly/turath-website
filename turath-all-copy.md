Turath Egypt fabricates bespoke architectural solid brass and red copper works, including custom chandeliers and wall sconces, hand-chiseled arabesque mirrors, sculptural console tables, solid brass door pulls, curved staircase balustrades, and hand-hammered wall cladding for luxury residences, palaces, and boutique hotels worldwide.
