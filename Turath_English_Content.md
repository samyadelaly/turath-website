## 3. About Turath
- **Overview:** Turath is an Egyptian workshop specializing in handcrafted solid brass, pure copper, and custom architectural metalwork. Founded in 2012 by Samy Adel, it is headquartered in Historic Cairo near Bab El-Nasr in the Al-Gamaliya district.
- **Our Craft:** We unite authentic Egyptian craftsmanship with contemporary design to create bespoke statement pieces for residential, hospitality, and commercial spaces, meticulously handcrafted by master artisans using generations-old techniques.
- **Our Vision:** Preserving the timeless art of hand-forged and cast metalwork through innovation, precision, and enduring design.
- **Tagline:** Turath — Authentic Egyptian Handcrafted Metalwork.
